# Privacy Policy for Bean and Nothingness Website

**Last Updated**: December 2024

## Introduction

RBOR Games ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Bean and Nothingness website at beanandnothingness.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
When you use forms on our website (if implemented), we may collect:
- **Name**: For personalization and communication
- **Email address**: For responding to inquiries
- **Message content**: Any information you choose to share with us

### Automatically Collected Information
When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:
- **Usage data**: Pages visited, time spent, referring website
- **Device information**: Browser type, operating system, device type
- **Log data**: IP address, access times, error logs

### Cookies and Tracking
We use the following tracking technologies:
- **Google Analytics**: To understand how visitors use our site (see Google Analytics section below)
- **Essential cookies**: For basic website functionality
- **No advertising cookies**: We do not use cookies for targeted advertising

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:
- **Communication**: Responding to your inquiries and messages
- **Website improvement**: Understanding how visitors use our site
- **Analytics**: Measuring website performance and user engagement
- **Technical support**: Troubleshooting and fixing issues
- **Legal compliance**: Meeting legal requirements and protecting our rights

We do NOT:
- Sell your personal information
- Use your information for targeted advertising
- Share your information with third parties except as described below

### Security Measures
We implement security measures including:
- **HTTPS encryption**: All data transmitted over secure connections
